# Field Kit for Cavalry

Pack of useful little filters for Cavalry, some ported from open source GLSL shaders.

### [Download now →](https://github.com/sammularczyk/cavalry-field-kit/releases/download/v1.0.0/Field.Kit.1.0.zip)
_Drag and drop the ZIP on your Cavalry Viewport to install._

## List of filters

### Ink and print
- **Print** — riso, screen print and newsprint
- **Hatching**
- **Photocopy**

### Broadcast and CRT
- **VHS**
- **Composite Video** — NTSC, PAL and PAL-M
- **Display Simulation** — CRT/LCD emulation

### Glow and light
- **Ultra Glow** — bloom and directional streaks
- **Godray**
- **Lens Flare**
- **Light Leak**
- **Lightwrap**

### Lens and blur
- **Lens Distortion** — distortion, aberration, blur and scatter

### Screens and displays
- **LED**
- **ASCII**

### Paint
- **Paint** — brush, oil and watercolour

### Colour and film
- **Film Emulation** — dial in Fuji recipes, grade, grain and damage
- **Film Burn**

### Geometry
- **Polar Coordinates**
